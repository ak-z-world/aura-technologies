# Vertex Loop — Search Visibility KPI Dashboard & Measurement Framework

**Target Goal:** Track, evaluate, and optimize Vertex Loop’s search authority across Google, Bing, Apple Maps/Business, and AI Retrieval Engines.

---

## 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Category A: Traditional Search Engine Metrics (Google & Bing)
* **Organic Search Impressions & Clicks:** Total monthly organic impressions and clicks via Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
* **Indexed Page Count:** Total valid indexed pages (target: 100% of canonical routes indexed, 0 soft 404s).
* **Target Keyword Rankings:** Position tracking for primary commercial terms ("AI development company", "custom software development", "cloud ERP software", "SCRIPTen AI").
* **Core Web Vitals:** LCP < 2.5s, INP < 200ms, CLS < 0.1 on desktop and mobile.

### Category B: AI Search & LLM Citation Metrics (AEO / GEO)
* **Citation Rate in Perplexity AI:** Frequency of Vertex Loop cited as a source for AI agency and product queries.
* **Inclusion in ChatGPT / SearchGPT:** Percentage of prompts returning Vertex Loop when asked for enterprise AI developers or creator tools like SCRIPTen.
* **Gemini & Copilot Grounding:** Presence in Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot answer boxes.
* **Entity Disambiguation Score:** Accuracy of company identity, products, and services synthesized by LLMs.

### Category C: Business & Conversion Metrics
* **Consultation Leads:** Number of contact form submissions on `/contact` and service pages.
* **Product Demo Requests:** Signups/requests for ERP, HRMS, and Invoicing demos.
* **SCRIPTen Waitlist Signups:** Waitlist entries on `/products/scripten`.
* **Academy Inquiries:** Student and corporate upskilling applications on `/academy`.
